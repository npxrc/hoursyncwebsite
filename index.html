Why HourSync

Designed to make submitting eHours effortless.

HourSync anticipates the friction in your workflow—speeding up logins, protecting your progress, and keeping everything feeling familiar.

Familiar by design

Built with Microsoft's WinUI 3 for a clean, native experience that feels right at home on Windows.

Secure by default

Credentials stay in the Windows Credential Manager, so Auto Login works without storing passwords in the app itself.†

Made for students

Crafted by students for students, with shortcuts and guidance that match how you already work.

Features

Boost your productivity.◊

Three core experiences keep you focused on the request instead of the portal.

Auto Login

Sign in once and HourSync will sign you in automatically every time, so you can jump straight to submitting.†

Drafts

HourSync saves each request as you go, so an accidental sign-out never means starting over.

Optional AI Assistant

Bring your preferred AI sidebar along to help you polish your request, or disable the feature completely if you prefer to work without it.

And many more features...

Trip Calculator

Automatically calculate the duration of trips and customize it based on your trip.

Request Splitting

Automatically split larger requests into smaller ones so you can submit large projects or trips for eHours.

Localization

Experience HourSync in English and German, with more languages coming soon.*
*I gotta find a translator bro

Automatic Request Grouping

HourSync automatically recognizes similar requests and groups them together on the home page.

Leaderboard

See how your eHours compare to your peers on the official leaderboard.

HEIC to PNG Converter

HourSync automatically converts HEIC images to PNG format when submitting your request so your instructor and you can see it.

Save and Load Drafts

Not only can you save your progress, but you can also load previous drafts to continue working on them.

Save to OneDrive

Save drafts to OneDrive for easy access and backup.
